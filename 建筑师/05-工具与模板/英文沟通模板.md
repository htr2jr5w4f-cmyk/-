# 英文沟通模板

## 摘要
本文是我们以持牌 builder 身份对外发英文邮件和信函时可以直接套用的模板，共 16 个，对象包括 certifier、council、设计师和工程师、业主、分包商、供应商、邻居和银行。每个模板都写明何时用、法定或合同依据（只引用本库或本次已读原文核实的条款）和中文说明，英文正文里全部用 [方括号占位符]，不含真实个人信息。用好这些模板的好处是：约检、变更、延期、付款这类事有严格的书面和时限要求，格式对了，就不会因为漏写一项而丢掉延期、收不到款或被 certifier 退回。术语查《中英建筑术语对照表.md》，合同管理的详细规则见《合同管理实务-进度款变更延期与裁决.md》。

适用州：QLD（布里斯班）｜资料核对日期：2026-11-21｜状态：部分待证实（法定和合同依据已读原文；邻居告知、dilapidation、银行放款、RFQ 属行业做法，标「行业做法」或「待证实」）

---

## 使用前必读
1. **先判断适用哪套规则**（详见《合同管理实务-进度款变更延期与裁决.md》第 0 节）：
   - 与**自住业主（resident owner）**签的住宅合同：按 QBCC Act Schedule 1B 和 QBCC New Home Construction Contract（下称 NHC）办，**不适用** BIF Act 第 3 章（s61(2)(b)）[S1][S4]。
   - **分包合同、商业合同、非自住业主**：适用 BIF Act（payment claim / payment schedule），分包合同还受 QBCC Act Part 4A 约束（书面合同、指示、保留金）[S2][S4]。
2. **工作日算法不同**：NHC 的 Business Day 只排除周末和昆州假日，圣诞期间照算 [S3]；BIF Act 的 business day 还排除 12 月 22–24 日、27–31 日和 1 月 2–10 日 [S7]。算截止日时注明用哪一套。
3. **送达**：NHC 通知须书面，可亲手交、预付邮资邮寄、传真或发到对方 "Last Email Address"（GC 30）[S3]。邮件写清合同编号，保留已发送记录。
4. **占位符**：[Project Address]、[Lot/Plan]、[Contract No.]、[Builder Company]、[QBCC Licence No.]、[Name]、[Phone]、[Email]、[Date] 等，发出前全部替换。不要把业主或他人的个人信息写进模板库。
5. **签名块**（所有模板通用，下文用 [Signature Block] 代替）：

```
Kind regards,
[Name]
[Position], [Builder Company]
QBCC Licence No. [QBCC Licence No.]
[Phone] | [Email]
```

## T1. 给 certifier：约检通知（Notice for inspection）
- **何时用**：每个法定检查阶段完成时（独立 Class 1a：开挖后、基础钢筋模板就位后、底板钢筋模板就位后、框架、配筋砌体填芯前、完工）[S6]。
- **依据**：Building Regulation 2021 s48——builder 须通知 certifier 某阶段已完成，可书面或口头；s51——certifier 须安排在与 builder 约定的时间检查，不得无理拒绝；s49——拿到检查证书前不得开始下一阶段；s46——开挖、基础、底板、final 只能由 building certifier 签证 [S6]。
- **中文说明**：口头约也合法，但一定要发书面确认，留证据。写清阶段名称（用 s44 的原话），附钢筋照片和相关证书，能减少复检。

```
Subject: Notice for Inspection – [Stage] – [Project Address] – BA Ref [Approval No.]

Dear [Certifier Name],

Under section 48 of the Building Regulation 2021, we give notice that the following stage of the assessable building work at [Project Address] ([Lot/Plan]) has been completed and is ready for inspection:

Stage: [e.g. "after the placement of formwork and reinforcement for the slab but before the concrete for the slab is poured"]
Building approval: [Approval No.] dated [Date]

Proposed inspection time: [Day, Date, Time] (alternative: [Day, Date, Time]).
Please confirm a time that suits you. We will not proceed to the next stage until we receive the certificate of inspection for this stage.

Attached:
- Photos of [reinforcement / excavation / frame] ([number] photos)
- [Engineer's inspection certificate (Form 12) / Form 15 / truss certification], if applicable
- [Termite management system details], if applicable

Site contact: [Site Supervisor Name], [Phone]. Site access: [gate code / instructions].

[Signature Block]
```

## T2. 给 certifier：提交资料
- **何时用**：申请审批时补资料、各阶段检查前提交工程师或分包的证书、final 检查前提交全部证书。
- **依据**：检查人员可以依赖 aspect inspection certificate（Form 12）或 QBCC licensee certificate（Form 43）来认定某一方面合格；但独立 Class 1a 的**最终阶段不能全部**靠 aspect 证书（Building Regulation s52(4)(5)）[S6]。表格名称和编号见《审批流程与法定检查阶段.md》[S9]。
- **中文说明**：每份文件写清表格号和签发人；final 前用清单一次交齐，避免 certifier 来回要。

```
Subject: Documents for [Stage / Final Inspection] – [Project Address] – BA Ref [Approval No.]

Dear [Certifier Name],

Please find attached the following documents for [stage] at [Project Address]:

| No. | Document | Issued by | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form 15 – Compliance certificate for building design or specification ([structural / other]) | [RPEQ Name/Company] | [Date] |
| 2 | Form 43 – Aspect Certificate (QBCC Licensee) – [waterproofing / termite management] | [Contractor, Licence No.] | [Date] |
| 3 | Form 12 – Aspect Inspection Certificate – [aspect] | [Competent person] | [Date] |
| 4 | [Truss / frame design certification] | [Manufacturer] | [Date] |
| 5 | [Energy efficiency / glazing compliance documents] | [Assessor] | [Date] |
| 6 | [Plumbing final inspection certificate (council)] | [Council] | [Date] |
| 7 | [Electrical certificate of testing and safety] | [Electrical contractor, Licence No.] | [Date] |

Please let us know if any further information is required before [inspection / issue of the final inspection certificate].

[Signature Block]
```

## T3. 给 certifier：问询（变更图纸、做法确认）
- **何时用**：现场要改与批准图纸不同的做法、对 NCC 理解有疑问、想用 Performance Solution。
- **依据**：检查须核对工作是否符合 building development approval（Building Regulation s52(2)、s53）[S6]；与批准图纸不符，检查会不合格并收到 noncompliance notice（s54）[S6]。是否需要修改审批，由 certifier 书面答复（修改审批的法定程序本文未核，待证实）。
- **中文说明**：先问后做。问题写成「是 / 否」可回答的形式，并要求书面答复。

```
Subject: Query – [Topic] – [Project Address] – BA Ref [Approval No.]

Dear [Certifier Name],

We seek your written advice on the following matter before proceeding:

1. Background: [Approved drawing No. / detail reference] shows [approved detail].
2. Proposed change / question: [Proposed detail, with reason, e.g. product unavailable].
3. Supporting information: [Engineer's advice / manufacturer's installation guide / CodeMark certificate / marked-up drawing], attached.

Questions:
(a) Is the proposed detail acceptable under the current building approval? Yes / No
(b) If not, what is required (e.g. amended approval, Form 15, Performance Solution report)?

We will hold this part of the work until we receive your reply. Our programme requires a response by [Date] to avoid delay.

[Signature Block]
```

## T4. 给 council：给排水检查预约（布里斯班）
- **何时用**：给排水 permit work 到达检查节点（覆盖前、许可条件写明的阶段、投入使用后 final）。
- **依据**：Plumbing and Drainage Regulation 2019 s63（约检时点，例如任何给排水被覆盖前；投入使用后 5 个工作日内约 final）、s65（未检查不得覆盖）[S9]。BCC：由 plumbing contractor 打 Plumbing Hotline 预约，**至少提前 48 小时**；检查前须已有 plumbing permit、已交 **Form 7**（responsible person）、管道外露并在试压状态、已缴费；新建住宅含 5 次检查，前一工作日下午 2 点后取消也计次 [S8]；final 前每个 testable backflow device 交 Form 9，卫生排水检查时交 as-constructed drainage plan [S9]。
- **中文说明**：预约是 **plumber 打电话**，不是 builder 发邮件。我们用这个模板做两件事：①给 plumber 发书面「请约检」指示；②约好后发给 council 的确认或补交文件邮件。预约次数有限，确认准备好再约。

```
(a) Builder to plumbing contractor

Subject: Please book council plumbing inspection – [Stage] – [Project Address]

Hi [Plumber Name],

[Stage, e.g. in-ground sanitary drainage] at [Project Address] will be ready for inspection on [Date]. Please book the council inspection (minimum 48 hours' notice) and confirm the booking time to us.

Before the inspection please make sure:
- Permit No. [Plumbing Permit No.] is issued and the Form 7 (responsible person) has been lodged;
- the work is exposed and under test;
- the as-constructed sanitary drainage plan is ready (for the drainage inspection);
- [Form 9 for each testable backflow prevention device], for the final inspection.

Please do not cover any work until it has passed inspection.

[Signature Block]

(b) Builder to council (document lodgement / confirmation)

Subject: Plumbing Permit [Permit No.] – [Project Address] – [Document / Inspection booking ref]

Dear Plumbing Compliance Team,

For plumbing permit [Permit No.] at [Project Address], please find attached [as-constructed sanitary drainage plan / Form 9 / commissioning certificate]. The plumbing contractor, [Plumbing Company, Licence No.], has booked the [stage] inspection for [Date, Time] (booking ref [Ref]).

[Signature Block]
```

## T5. 给 council：占道许可问询（人行道、道路）
- **何时用**：要在人行道堆料、放 skip bin、设临时围栏、开挖人行道接雨水、车辆跨越路缘，或要封车道、吊装进出场。
- **依据**（BCC 页面）[S5][S10]：
  - 在人行道堆放建材设备、设临时围栏、开挖人行道或道路、跨越非车道路缘、接 council 雨水管等**须事先批准**；有些还须 permit。结果约需 **20 天**。
  - 在 CBD 以外、非州管主干道上，人行道使用或堆料**不超过 48 小时**的不需批准；住宅区须始终留出 **1.2 m** 通行宽度。
  - 必交：标明工作位置、临时构筑物尺寸、土方位置和规模、行人绕行方式的场地草图；可附批准图纸、照片、交通管理计划。
  - 封车道或道路：至少提前 **20 个完整工作日**申请 temporary road closure certificate of assessment，批准后再向 Queensland Police Service 申请 permit；6:30–18:00 以外施工的封路须先有 EP Act 批准。
- **中文说明**：费用按地块所在 zone 计（BCC 页面有表，以页面为准）。正式申请通过 BCC 在线表格，这封邮件只用来事先确认需要哪种批准。

```
Subject: Enquiry – Footpath / Road Use for Construction – [Project Address]

Dear Brisbane City Council,

We are the licensed builder for a [new dwelling / renovation] at [Project Address] ([Lot/Plan]), building approval [Approval No.]. We plan the following temporary activities:

| Activity | Location | Size | Dates / Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Deposit building materials / skip bin on footway] | [Frontage, e.g. 3 m from western boundary] | [L x W m] | [From – To] |
| [Temporary construction fencing on footway] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| [Lane closure for crane / concrete pump] | [Street name] | [ ] | [Date, Time] |
| [Excavate footway to connect to council stormwater] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

A pedestrian clear path of at least 1.2 m will be maintained at all times.

Could you please confirm:
1. Which approvals or permits are required for each activity (Application/Notice to work on Council property, footway permit, Temporary Lane/Road Closure assessment);
2. The applicable zone and fees;
3. Any additional documents required (e.g. traffic management plan, pedestrian management plan).

A site plan sketch is attached.

[Signature Block]
```

## T6. RFI（向设计师或工程师问询）
- **何时用**：图纸不清、互相矛盾、缺尺寸、现场条件与图纸不符。
- **依据**：行业做法；RFI 本身不是变更。答复如果改变了合同工作或价格，与业主之间仍须走变更手续（QBCC Act Sch 1B s40–41；NHC GC 21）[S1][S3]；答复导致延误的，按 EOT 条款另行申请（Sch 1B s42；GC 23）[S1][S3]。
- **中文说明**：一个 RFI 只问一件事，给出我们建议的做法和需要答复的日期，并写明对造价和工期的可能影响，日后好据此申请变更或延期。编号登记进 RFI 登记册（见《高层-工地管理计划模板.md》）。

```
Subject: RFI No. [###] – [Short Title] – [Project Name / Address]

To: [Architect / Designer / Engineer Name, Company]
From: [Builder Company]
Date issued: [Date]          Response required by: [Date]

Reference documents: [Drawing No. / Rev / Detail / Spec clause]

Question:
[Describe the issue clearly. E.g. "Drawing S-03 Rev B shows edge beam 450 deep; Drawing A-10 Rev C section shows 300. Please confirm the correct depth."]

Builder's proposed solution (for your consideration):
[Proposed detail]

Potential impact:
Cost: [None / To be advised / Estimated $ ]
Time: [None / Affects [activity] from [Date]]

Attachments: [Marked-up drawing / photos]

Please note that any response that changes the scope, cost or time will be treated as a variation and/or extension of time under the contract.

[Signature Block]
```

## T7. 变更单（对应 NHC Form 5 – Variation Document）
- **何时用**：任何增加、减少或替换合同工作的情况，在变更工作开始**之前**。
- **依据**：
  - 必填内容（QBCC Act Sch 1B s41；NHC GC 21.3）：清楚可读；描述变更；请求日期；如有延误，builder 对延误期的合理估计；合同价变化或计算方法；加价的何时支付；减价的何时扣减 [S1][S3]。
  - 业主书面同意前不得开工（s40(5)）；双方同意后 5 个工作日内且在开工前交签字副本（s40(2)；GC 21.5）[S1][S3]。
  - 加价：法律规定变更工作开始前不得收（s41(3)），NHC 更严，**变更工作完成后**才能要求付款（GC 21.4）[S1][S3]。
  - builder 提出的变更，只有不可合理预见、或可预见但因签约时 builder 无法控制的情况才有权加价（GC 21.7）；法定要求的变更须业主事先书面同意（GC 21.8）；额外开挖和基础须在知道后 5 个 Business Days 内书面通知（GC 21.10）[S3]。
- **中文说明**：变更单本身用 QBCC Form 5 或本库《变更单variation模板.md》。下面是随变更单发出的英文邮件和字段对照，确保 7 项一项不少。

| Form 5 / s41 字段 | 英文写法 |
|---|---|
| 变更编号、合同信息 | Variation No. [##]; Contract [No.] dated [Date]; Site [Project Address] |
| 描述 | Description of variation: [location, item, specification, quantity; drawing/photo refs] |
| 请求日期 | Date of request for variation: [Date]; requested by [Owner / Builder / Certifier requirement] |
| 延误估计 | Contractor's reasonable estimate of delay: [## Business Days / Nil] |
| 价格变化 | Change to Contract Price: [+/- $ incl. GST] or method of calculation: [rates / cost plus [##]% margin] |
| 何时支付 | If increase, to be paid: [with the progress claim for the [Stage] Stage after the variation work is completed] |
| 何时扣减 | If decrease, to be accounted for: [in the next progress claim] |
| 签署 | Signed by Contractor and Owner, with dates |

```
Subject: Variation Document No. [##] for your approval – [Project Address] – Contract [No.]

Dear [Owner Name],

Please find attached Variation Document No. [##] (QBCC Form 5) for [short description].

Summary:
- Requested by: [you / us / the certifier] on [Date]
- Change to the Contract Price: [+ $ incl. GST] (calculation attached)
- Estimated delay: [## Business Days / Nil]
- Payment: [the increase will be claimed with the [Stage] progress claim after the variation work is completed]

We will not start the variation work until you have signed and returned the Variation Document. We will give you a signed copy within 5 Business Days after it is agreed and before the work starts.

If you do not wish to proceed, please let us know by [Date] so the work can continue as originally contracted.

[Signature Block]
```

## T8. EOT 申请和回复
### T8a. builder 向业主申请（对应 NHC Form 2）
- **何时用**：出现可索延期的延误事件后，在知道（或理应知道）延误原因和程度后 **10 个 Business Days** 内。
- **依据**：住宅合同只能因①不可合理预见且非 builder 可控的延误、②业主造成的延误、③符合 s40 的变更，书面申请、业主书面批准；不按规定申请的不得依赖延期；业主批准后 5 个工作日内给签字副本（QBCC Act Sch 1B s42）[S1]。NHC 还允许 Schedule Item 6B 所列延误事件超过约定天数的部分（GC 23.1(a)(iii)），须用 Form 2 写明延误原因和申请延长的日期（GC 23.1(b)）；builder 须采取合理措施减少延误（GC 23.2）[S3]。
- **中文说明**：每个事件单独申请，附天气记录、照片、日志或往来邮件；算的是对 **Date for Practical Completion** 的影响，不是对某个工序的影响。

```
Subject: Extension of Time Claim No. [##] – [Project Address] – Contract [No.]

Dear [Owner Name],

Under Condition 23 of the Contract and section 42 of Schedule 1B of the QBCC Act, we claim an extension of the Date for Practical Completion as set out in the attached QBCC Form 2 – Extension of Time Claim and Owner's Response to Claim.

1. Cause of delay: [e.g. inclement weather exceeding the allowance in Schedule Item 6B / variation No. [##] / delay in owner selections / latent rock not reasonably foreseeable]
2. Category (Condition 23.1(a)): [(i) variation complying with Condition 21 / (ii) delay caused by the Owner / (iii) delay event in Schedule Item 6B exceeding the stated allowance / (iv) other cause not reasonably foreseeable and beyond our reasonable control]
3. Dates of delay: [From – To]
4. Date we became aware of the cause and extent of the delay: [Date]
5. Extension claimed: [##] Business Days
6. Current Date for Practical Completion: [Date]; proposed new Date for Practical Completion: [Date]
7. Steps taken to reduce the delay (Condition 23.2): [e.g. re-sequenced trades, extra crew]
8. Supporting evidence: [site diary entries, BOM rainfall records, photos, correspondence]

Under Condition 23.4, please assess this claim and return the signed Form 2 within 10 Business Days of receipt, either agreeing to the extension or giving reasons for rejecting all or part of it.

[Signature Block]
```

### T8b. 业主回复字段（供业主或业主代表填写；我们作为 builder 可附在 T8a 后）
- **依据**：业主须在收到后 10 个 Business Days 内合理评估并交回 Form 2，同意或写明拒绝理由，逾期视为有争议（GC 23.4）；业主不同意不会让完工日失效，但 builder 可就无理拒绝索赔损失（GC 23.5）[S3]。

```
Owner's Response to EOT Claim No. [##]
[ ] I/We agree to the extension of [##] Business Days. New Date for Practical Completion: [Date]
[ ] I/We agree to [##] Business Days only, for the following reasons: [reasons]
[ ] I/We reject the claim for the following reasons: [reasons]
Owner signature: ______________  Name: [Name]  Date: [Date]
```

### T8c. builder 回复分包商的延期申请
- **依据**：按分包合同的延期条款（各合同不同，行业做法）；分包合同须书面（QBCC Act s67G）[S2]。我们给业主的 EOT 有 10 个 Business Days 硬时限，所以分包合同应要求分包商更早通知。
- **中文说明**：同意、部分同意或拒绝都要写理由；如果是业主原因导致，同时启动 T8a。

```
Subject: Response to your EOT Claim [Ref] – [Project Address] – Subcontract [No.]

Dear [Subcontractor Name],

We have assessed your claim dated [Date] for an extension of [##] working days for [scope].

Our assessment: [Approved in full / Approved for [##] days / Rejected]
Reasons: [e.g. the delay from [Date] to [Date] was caused by late delivery of your own materials, which is within your control under clause [##] of the Subcontract.]
Revised completion date for your works: [Date]

[If applicable: Please continue to take all reasonable steps to minimise the delay.]

[Signature Block]
```

## T9. 进度款申请和 payment schedule
### T9a. 向自住业主的进度款申请（NHC Form 3）
- **何时用**：完成合同 Schedule Item 8A/8B 的某一阶段时。
- **依据**：NHC GC 19.1：按阶段比例申请，并按 PC/PS 实际成本和已完成的变更调整；用 Form 3（或类似文件）记录调整；须附 **Form 4 – Notice of Dispute of Progress Claim**（空白，供业主有争议时用）和该阶段相关的检查证书；业主在 5 个 Business Days 内支付无争议部分，有争议的在 5 个 Business Days 内交回 Form 4 [S3]。PC 和 PS 项要附发票或收据（GC 4.10）[S3]。PC 阶段款还须附 Form 6 和 Form 7（GC 19.1(e)），见 T10a 之后 [S3]。BIF Act 第 3 章不适用（s61(2)(b)），**不要**在给自住业主的申请上写 "payment claim under the BIF Act" [S4]。
- **中文说明**：阶段名称和定义用合同原词（例如 Enclosed Stage，不写 lock-up）。

```
Subject: Progress Claim No. [##] – [Stage] Stage – [Project Address] – Contract [No.]

Dear [Owner Name],

We have completed the [Base / Frame / Enclosed / Fixing] Stage as defined in Condition 1 of the Contract. Please find attached:

1. QBCC Form 3 – Progress Claim No. [##]
2. QBCC Form 4 – Notice of Dispute of Progress Claim (for your use if you dispute any part of the claim)
3. Certificate(s) of inspection relevant to this stage: [Form 16 – [stage] inspection dated [Date]]
4. Invoices/receipts for Prime Cost Items / Provisional Sums included in this claim: [list]

Claim summary (incl. GST):
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Stage payment ([##]% of Contract Price) | $[ ] |
| PC item adjustments (incl. markup) | +/- $[ ] |
| Provisional sum adjustments (incl. markup) | +/- $[ ] |
| Completed variations not previously paid (Nos. [##]) | +/- $[ ] |
```

### T9c. Payment schedule（我们回应分包商或供应商的付款申请）
- **何时用**：收到任何 payment claim（含写有 "invoice" 的文件）后，在合同期限和 **15 个 business days** 中较早者内 [S7]。
- **依据**：BIF Act s69——注明所回应的申请、拟付金额，少付的写明理由（含扣款理由）；s76——时限，按拟付金额在到期日前付清；s77——不回就要在到期日付全额；**s82(4)——将来裁决时不得提出 payment schedule 里没写的新理由**，所以理由要写全 [S7]。分包合同付款期超过 25 个 business days 的条款无效（QBCC Act s67U）[S2][S4]。用保留金抵扣的，须在知道权利后 28 天内另发书面通知（s67J）[S2]。

## T10. 缺陷通知
### T10a. builder 回复业主的缺陷通知（完工后 12 个月内）
- **何时用**：业主在实际完工后 12 个月内书面通知缺陷时。
- **依据**：NHC GC 29：builder 须修复实际完工后 12 个月内业主发现的缺陷或遗漏；业主书面通知并提供合理进入；builder 在通知后 **20 个 Business Days** 内、在正常工作时间修复，有合理原因不能按时的，按约定时间或合理时间修复 [S3]。完工时的小缺陷按 Form 6（Defects Document）写明的时间修复（GC 28.7）[S3]。法定保修诉讼期和 Home Warranty 时限见 CLAUDE.md 第五节 [S9]。
- **中文说明**：不同意的项也要回复并写理由，可引用 QBCC Standards and Tolerances Guide（见《QBCC允许偏差与验收标准.md》）。拒绝让 builder 回来整改，业主可能失去保险索赔资格 [S9]，所以要书面记录约访经过。

## T12. 给分包商的发包函（Letter of Award / Subcontract）
- **何时用**：选定分包商后、开工前。
- **依据**：
  - builder 与分包商的合同属于 QBCC Act Part 4A 的 building contract（不是住宅合同）[S2][S4]。工程合理造价超过 $10,000 的，**开工前**须书面；书面合同须写明：工作范围、完工时间、价款或计算方法、双方关于保留金和担保的约定、承包方名称、执照号（照执照所示）、工程地址（s67G）[S2]。
  - 保留金和担保：实际完工前合计不超过分包价 5%（s67L）、每次付款扣留不超过 10%（s67M）、完工后不超过 2.5%（s67N）；未写明释放时间的，完工 12 个月后释放（s67NA）；缺陷责任期结束前 10 个 business days 内须发 approved form 通知（s67NC）[S2]。
  - 付款：付款期超过 25 个 business days 的条款无效（s67U）；pay when paid 条款无效（BIF Act s74）[S2][S7]。
  - 高风险建筑工作开工前须有 SWMS（WHS Reg s299–303）[S9]。
- **中文说明**：发包函附工作范围（可用《各工种工作范围scope模板.md》）。签约前在 QBCC 网站查分包商执照类别和状态。

## T15. 给邻居的施工告知和 dilapidation 邀请
- **何时用**：开工前 1–2 周；有开挖、打桩、振动大的工序或靠近边界施工时，尤其要做 dilapidation（施工前房屋现状记录）。
- **依据**：
  - 施工噪音时间：布里斯班 6:30–18:30（周一至周六），周日和公众假期不得施工（EP Act s440R）[S9]。
